# PrivateVox 0.2.0 alpha 1

Local voice dictation. Hold a key, talk, let go: the words appear in whatever app is in front. Whisper runs on your own GPU and nothing leaves your machine.

This is the first alpha. It is for trying out, not for relying on. **macOS on Apple Silicon** is the platform it was built and tested on. **Windows** and **Linux** builds come from our automated build and have not been run by a person yet; please report what happens.

## Install

### macOS (Apple Silicon, macOS 12 or newer)
1. Download `PrivateVox_0.2.0_aarch64.dmg`, open it, drag PrivateVox to Applications.
2. The build is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate. On first launch macOS will refuse to open it. Right-click the app → Open → Open, or run once in Terminal:
   `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PrivateVox.app`
3. Launch. A small pill appears at the bottom of the screen above the Dock. Click it to open the panel and go to the **Setup** tab.
4. Grant three permissions from the buttons there: **Microphone** (to record), **Accessibility** (to paste into other apps), **Input Monitoring** (to see the Right Option key). After granting Accessibility or Input Monitoring, quit PrivateVox from the menu bar and start it again.
5. The first launch downloads the Whisper model (574 MB) with a progress bar. Then hold **Right Option**, speak, release.

### Windows (x64, 64-bit Windows 10/11, a Vulkan-capable GPU)
1. Download the `.exe` installer (NSIS) or the `.msi`. SmartScreen will warn because the build is unsigned: choose "More info" → "Run anyway".
2. Launch. Hold **Right Alt** to talk, or tap **Ctrl+Shift+Space** to toggle. No permission prompts on Windows.
3. Untested on real hardware as of this release; expected to work on AMD RX 6000/7000/9000, NVIDIA and Intel Arc through Vulkan.

### Linux (x64, X11)
1. Download the `.AppImage` (make it executable) or the `.deb`.
2. Needs a Vulkan-capable GPU and driver, PulseAudio or PipeWire, and X11 for the hold key. Wayland is not supported in this alpha.
3. Untested as of this release.

## What you get
- Push-to-talk, toggle, or hold-or-toggle; rebindable hotkeys with a key recorder.
- Whisper large-v3-turbo by default; large-v3 and distil-large-v3 downloadable from the panel; per-model benchmark.
- A memory strip that shows what fits on your GPU and recommends a model.
- Long dictations decoded in chunks while you speak, up to five minutes.
- History with search, edit, pin, paste again, raw view, export.
- Undo last paste. Idle unload of the model. Clean shutdown that never strands GPU memory.

## What is missing (next phase)
- LLM clean-up of the transcript (filler words, punctuation, email / bullet / code modes), locally via Ollama and on another machine on your LAN.
- Personal dictionary, multiple spoken languages, keeping audio clips, a signed and notarised build.

## Hardware notes
- Apple Silicon with 8 GB: turbo is the right model; large-v3 fits but leaves little room.
- Windows with an 8 GB card: turbo. 16 GB: any of the three.
- A dictation takes about one second on an M1 after the first one; the very first launch after an install compiles GPU shaders once (about 10 s).
